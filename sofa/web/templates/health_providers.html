{{ rule(2, "What a front desk shows") }}

Each front desk page is the provider's own view: its places with the queue now, and its appointment book. A booking made on a call appears here a moment later. The book shows each caller only as a short reference, because the provider never sees a phone number. Where a hospital has no computer system, this page is the appointment book it did not have.

{{ rule(3, "Simulated, and built for the real thing") }}

The four providers on this site are simulated: their places, queues, fees, stock and patients are made up. They are built to the same contract a real provider uses, with their own records, their own secret and signed requests, so connecting a real hospital means a new connection and real data, not a new design. The patient guide of the cancer centre is sample content that the centre's own guide will replace.
