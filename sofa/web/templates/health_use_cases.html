Choosing a clinic

Caller: Book an appointment in Surulere.
Talk: I found three places near Surulere. The first is a private clinic, less than a kilometre away, about 40 minutes of waiting and 8,000 naira. The second is a state health centre, about 100 minutes of waiting and 500 naira. Which one would you like?
Caller: The cheaper one.
Talk: I can book you there tomorrow at 8 in the morning. Shall I book it?
Caller: Yes please.
Talk: Done. Your booking code is K K S T. I have also texted you the details.

A question for the care team

Caller: What should I do if I have a fever during chemotherapy?
Talk: Contact the centre straight away, or come to the emergency unit, if you have a high temperature or shivering, bleeding that will not stop, trouble breathing, or vomiting that does not stop. In an emergency call 112.

The text after a visit

Day before: Reminder: your appointment at the Ikeja care centre is tomorrow at 8 in the morning. Please arrive 15 minutes early.
After, if the patient came: Thank you for coming. If you have questions about your visit, call this number any time.
After, if not: We missed you. To book a new time, call this number and say book an appointment.

Services marked Live run today on simulated providers. Texts name a place by a discreet name, never by what is treated there.
