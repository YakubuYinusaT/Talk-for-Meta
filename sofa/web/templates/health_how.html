How it works

One call, one understanding step, and facts that come from the providers.

Talk hears and understands the caller. The providers hold the records. Talk never says anything about a clinic that the clinic's own system did not confirm.

{{ rule(2, "Principles") }}

Facts

The provider is the source

Every time, price, queue and stock level a caller hears comes from a provider's record. The model understands and words; it never decides a fact.

Choice

The caller decides

An area is not a hospital. Talk lays out the options and asks which one, and books only after a read-back and a clear yes.

Emergencies

They come first

Words like "not breathing" or "collapsed" get the emergency number, 112, at once, from any point in a call, and then the nearest emergency care. Talk gives general information and coordinates care. It does not diagnose.

Privacy

Private by design

A provider sees a different reference for the same caller, never a phone number. A text names a place by a discreet name, never by what is treated there, and the provider keeps the booking. Talk keeps the caller's number, what was said on the call and the booking details; the demo page shows numbers masked.

{{ rule(3, "How a provider connects") }}

By web API

A provider answers a small set of signed requests: find places, list free times, book, cancel, report what became of a visit.

By appointment book

A provider with no system uses the book Talk keeps for it. Its front desk page shows who is coming and marks who came.

Nothing in Talk is written for any one provider. The four providers on this site are simulated, built to the same contract a real one uses. Meet them.

{{ rule(4, "The patient guide") }}

A centre can give Talk its own patient guide. Talk answers a caller's basic question only from the guide's entries, never from the model's memory. A warning sign, such as a fever during treatment, is always answered first and exactly as the centre wrote it. A question the guide does not cover goes to the care team instead of being guessed at.
